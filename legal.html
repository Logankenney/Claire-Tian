legal and privacy

effective 26 november 2026

1. who we are

www.claire-tian.com is run by claire tian, a photography business owned by claire tian in new york, ny, usa ("claire tian", "we", "us"). it is responsible for this site and for the information described here. to reach us, use contact or write to hello@claire-tian.com.

2. what this covers

this page is our privacy notice and the terms of use for the site. by using the site you agree to these terms. if you don't agree, please don't use it.

3. what we collect on the site

the site keeps a visitor log. for each visit it records: the page, the site you came from, any campaign tags in the link, device type, browser, operating system, screen size, language, time zone, how long the page was open, how far you scrolled, and clicks on photos, ig and send. it also records the time of the visit and the page's load time.

your ip address and browser details are used for a moment to make a visitor code, which is a one-way code that changes every day. the ip address itself is not stored, and the key that makes each day's code is deleted the next day, so a code can't be traced back to you or linked across days.

no cookies. nothing is stored on your device, except a single setting if you choose to switch counting off (see section 8).

4. what we don't collect

no names, email addresses, phone numbers, precise location, payment details or anything you type into the site. the "i have an idea" box is not sent to us by the site: "send" opens your own email app, and we only receive what you choose to email.

5. email and messages

if you email or message us, we receive your name, address and whatever you send, and keep it to reply, to run the business and for our records. what you send is not confidential, and ideas and material you share are covered by our engagement and intellectual property terms (section 13).

6. why we use it

to see which pages and photographs people look at, where visitors come from, whether the site works on their devices, and how to improve the site and its search ranking. this is our legitimate interest in running and improving the site. we also use it to write a weekly report for claire tian.

7. who else handles it

we never sell or share your information for advertising. service providers handle it for us: github pages hosts the site and, like any web host, receives your ip address to deliver pages; supabase stores the visitor log; google fonts serves the typeface and receives your ip address when the page loads it; google workspace holds email and the weekly reports. instagram is a separate site with its own privacy policy once you follow the "ig" link. we may disclose information if the law requires it or to protect our rights.

8. your choices

browsers that send a global privacy control signal are not counted. you can also switch counting off for your browser by opening any page with ?notrack at the end of the address, and on again with ?track. blocking scripts also stops it, and the site still works.

9. how long we keep it

visitor log records are deleted after 400 days. weekly reports contain totals only and are kept as business records. emails are kept as long as needed for the reason they were sent and for our records.

10. your rights

depending on where you live (for example california and other us states, the eu, the uk), you may have the right to know what we hold about you, get a copy, correct it, delete it, object to or restrict its use, and not be treated differently for using these rights. because the visitor log holds no name, contact details or ip address, we usually can't link a record to you, and we will tell you if that is the case. to ask, use contact. you can also complain to your local data protection authority.
