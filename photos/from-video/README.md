# from video

Stills taken from Claire's Instagram reels (instagram.com/maisonsumika), for use as extra photos on the site.
They are not on the site yet: nothing in this folder is listed in `photos.json`. To use one, copy it into `photos/`
and run `python3 tools/photos.py`.

- Source: the five reels filed in Jarvis Document Control, "Claire's Posts" (videos part 1 and 2, 2026-11-25).
- Each still is a full frame from the reel, cropped to the middle at 2 by 3 portrait, in its original colour.
- Left out: any frame with text in it (titles, captions, signs, watermarks), posed portraits, and landscape clips
  shown letterboxed in a reel. That removed every frame of "analog asmr" and "japan matsuri".
- Portugal stills are 720 by 1080 (the reel was 720p); the rest are 1080 by 1620.
